# voice.md — token-tracker / Florin

Binding brand voice for README, docs, and in-product copy. Product name stays **token-tracker**. Lore unit is **florin** / ticker **flr**.

Mood: mint-honest craft, quiet awe, **deadpan**, **sincere brand love**. Not SaaS keynote theater. Not purple Renaissance AI. Not crypto brochure. Not a bit that explains itself.

Spelling note: the comedian is **Conner** O’Malley (one *n*). Norm Macdonald inspired him too — anti-comedy, embrace being stupid, not clever.

## Deep dive — Conner O’Malley, *Stand Up Solutions*, love of brands

Primary text: [*Stand Up Solutions*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=…) (2024 YouTube special). Character: **Richard Eagleton**, Des Plaines founder/CEO, investor keynote + product demo for **KENN** (Kinetic Emotional Neural Network), “the world’s first AI standup comedian that is powered by 5G.”

Conner has said the show started with a **polo shirt on CustomInk** and watching a lot of **Shark Tank** — brand identity before the product was real. He plays Richard with love (“you can’t shit on them”); desires are Midwest-humble (garage of RAV4s, “an all-brown house”), not Musk cosplay — “a guy that sells above-ground pools.”

### What Richard *loves* (steal this energy)

| Object of love | How he talks | Florin translation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Branded merch** | Polo + mesh hat with company mark; CustomInk first | Love the **F-coin / mural / ASCII disc** as real marks. Kit before manifesto. |
| **RAV4** | Interrupts the keynote for MSRP, MPG, USB “galore,” resale — unprompted, unpaid, sincere | Interrupt for the **ledger path**, the **icon**, the **zero deps** fact — affection for the object, not the pitch deck |
| **McDonald’s / Des Plaines** | Hometown brand history as identity (museum across from the real one) | Florence / florin as **hometown lore**, one dry paragraph — pride, not thesis |
| **Shark Tank form** | “Invest in the company / invest in me / become family” | We invert: invest **zero**; it’s local; no angels. Form awareness only. |
| **Specs recited like poetry** | “USB connectivity galore in this thing!” | Specs as love language: Node 22+, `~/.token-tracker/`, hosts by name |

**Brand love rule:** Treat florin marks and the local ledger the way Richard treats the RAV4 — specific, affectionate, slightly too much detail, never ironic detachment. Irony kills the mark.

### What Richard *performs* (steal the craft, invert the content)

| Keynote move | Richard’s version | Our version |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | Apocalypse → “we can change the world” → limitless intelligence | Skip. Open with mural + what it is. |
| Hype loop | “Yes! Wow! Oh my God!” / “we got so much to talk about” | Cut. Quiet. One install path. |
| Product name as spell | “powered by 5G for 100% accurate comedy” | Never unexplained buzzwords. Say the path. |
| Social proof theater | Angel wings in the audience; cash infusion “like dessert” | No. |
| Origin story | Des Plaines facts → veteran-ship → diet → *then* the product | Optional florin origin **after** proof shots; keep short |
| Empathy / data | Dirty box pulls texts, banks, photos via 5G | **Anti-KENN product truth:** local only, your machine, no harvest |
| Utopia pitch | “We can abolish conflict! We have the technology!” | Count tokens. That’s enough. |

**Form rule:** *Stand Up Solutions* is a product demo that never shuts up. High-star READMEs are product demos that shut up early. Same genre (show the thing). Opposite runtime.

### What we explicitly reject (anti-examples from the special)

Do not write like this, even as parody, in the README:

> Stand Up Solutions is on the precipice of an incredible moment. Perfect time for a cash infusion, although we don’t need it.

> By the time you leave tonight, you’re gonna be certified AI experts.

> Powered by 5G for 100% accurate comedy.

> Everyone was experiencing social cohesion. There were no stabbings.

> A group of 30 people in Silicon Valley made a geopolitical decision… yes!

> Ethical slavery / hacking slavery (even “as a joke”).

> Unlock limitless intelligence potential at our fingertips.

token-tracker is the **opposite product** of KENN: not harvested comedy, not investor theater, not phone-slurp. The O’Malley value we keep is **sincere love of a branded object + total commitment to the premise**, delivered with Norm-flat prose — not Richard’s volume.

### Conner craft maxims (for writers)

1. **Start with the shirt.** Mark / kit / mural can exist before the essay. (CustomInk.)
2. **Do it with love.** Don’t sneer at florin, at Max’s heat map, at the user. Love the clerk energy.
3. **Midwest product, not Valley god.** RAV4 USB ports > Elon. Local ledger > precipice.
4. **Be stupid on purpose.** Anti-clever. Norm kinship. Don’t intellectualize the joke in the README.
5. **Commit, then understate.** O’Malley escalation + Norm landing. Florin paragraph believes itself; last line is quieter than the setup (“We don’t move your gold. We count what you already spent.”).

## Do / Don't

**Do**

- Lead with what it is in one sentence
- Put proof (screenshots) before persuasion
- Ask Why bullets as real user questions
- Name hosts, paths, and flags exactly — specs as love language
- Keep florin lore to one short paragraph max on the README
- Prefer `is` / `has` / `runs` over “serves as” / “stands as”
- Match high-star CLI READMEs: ripgrep, fzf, bat, uv
- Love the mural / coin / ASCII disc out loud in craft docs; keep README quiet
- Write florin lore as if the mint clerk believes it; land Norm-flat
- Cut any line that exists only to sound clever or “excited”

**Don't**

- Write *Stand Up Solutions* keynote (precipice, cash infusion, certified experts, social cohesion)
- Inflate florin symbolism (“trusted settlement,” “assay mark,” “testament”)
- Sound like SaaS / crypto / purple Renaissance AI
- Sound like a comedian doing a tech bit (“folks,” rimshot rhythm)
- Name-drop Norm or Conner in the README (inspiration is for writers)
- Harvest / 5G / mind-reading metaphors for the product
- Stack short dramatic fragments for fake punch
- Use em dashes as style flourishes

## Lexicon

**Preferred:** ledger, history, snapshot, feature, host, estimated cost, zero deps, local, `~/.token-tracker/`, mark, coin, mural

**Banned:** precipice, cash infusion, certified expert, social cohesion, powered by 5G, trusted settlement, assay mark, pivotal, seamless, unlock, empower, journey, tapestry, cutting-edge, enterprise-grade, on-chain, reinvent, “folks,” “game-changer,” “let that sink in,” “yes! wow!”

**Person:** second person (“you”) for instructions; no corporate “we deliver”; no keynote “we’re so excited”

**Contractions:** yes in lore/prose; keep command blocks literal

**Emoji:** none in README headings or feature bullets

**Comedy mechanic:** understatement > punchline. Brand love shows as **specific detail**, not volume. If you must cut a joke or a fact, cut the joke.

## Writer’s test

Before shipping a line, ask:

1. Would Norm say this flatter?
2. Would Conner love the *object* harder (RAV4 / polo / path) and stop explaining?
3. Would Richard’s keynote say this louder? If yes, delete.
4. Would ripgrep delete the sentence?

If (3) is yes or (4) is yes, edit.
